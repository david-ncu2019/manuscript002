Because positive and negative monthly errors can partly cancel within the signed cumulative error, a small $R_{s,80}$ does not necessarily indicate that the absolute cumulative error stopped growing over the evaluation period; it indicates only that the average rate of accumulation over the full 80 months was small. Section~S4 had the largest horizon-normalized rate under every initial-record length, consistent with its comparatively large absolute cumulative errors in the main text.
