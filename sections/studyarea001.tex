\subsection{Tuku monitoring site}
\label{subsec:tuku_site}

The Tuku monitoring site lies in Yunlin County within the Choushui River Alluvial Fan of central Taiwan. Unconsolidated Quaternary deposits beneath the fan become progressively finer from the proximal area in the east toward the Taiwan Strait in the west \citep{survey_project_1999, liu_characterization_2004}. Tuku occupies the agricultural middle-to-distal part of the fan, where interbedded gravel, sand, silt, and clay form a sequence of aquifers and aquitards. Seasonal groundwater withdrawal for irrigation causes repeated hydraulic head declines and has contributed to persistent land subsidence in this area \citep{chang2022_wetanddry, lu2020_crfp}.

The Tuku site contains the four observation types required for the analysis. The MLCW provides depth-resolved compaction, nearby groundwater wells record hydraulic head at several screened intervals, the borehole log describes sediment composition with depth, and the TKJS cGNSS station measures vertical surface displacement. Their proximity permits a local comparison of hydraulic, subsurface, and surface deformation records without using observations from another MLCW site. \Cref{fig:tuku_monitoring_site} shows the study location and monitoring arrangement.
