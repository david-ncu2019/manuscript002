## Physical Impact

The well depth corrections directly affect the conversion of piezometric head measurements to groundwater level depth (GWL):

```
gwl_depth_m = elev_leveling_m - piezometric_head_m_msl
```

Where piezometric measurements are recorded at the screen interval specified in `well_screen_str`, well depth determines the maximum meaningful measurement depth. The critical ANNAN correction (201.0 m → 104.0 m) will prevent false positive detections of groundwater level changes at depths below 104 m at that well.
